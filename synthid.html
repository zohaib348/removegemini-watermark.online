What is Google DeepMind SynthID?

Developed by Google DeepMind, SynthID is a steganographic watermark technology that embeds subtle noise patterns into AI-generated imagery and audio. Unlike a visible logo, SynthID does not display text or graphic marks on top of your visual content.

Instead, it modifies pixel values in the frequency domain so that automated detection systems can spot AI generations. Our SynthID Remover Online applies phase-neutralizing filters and strips EXIF/C2PA metadata so your media remains clean.
