How Reverse Alpha Blending Works

When Google Gemini exports an image, it overlays a translucent 4-point sparkle logo using standard alpha composite blending:

I_watermarked = (α × C_logo) + ((1 - α) × I_original)

Standard AI image erasers try to guess what's underneath by blurring surrounding pixels. geminiwatermark-remover.online algebraically solves for I_original, retrieving the authentic pixel values with mathematical precision:

I_original = (I_watermarked - α × C_logo) / (1 - α)
