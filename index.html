Generative AI has transformed how designers, marketers, photographers, and content creators produce visual media. When generating imagery using Google's suite of AI tools—including Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini Flash, Imagen 3, and Google Veo—the output file automatically includes a translucent corner watermark. This watermark typically appears as a stylized 4-point sparkle logo overlaid in the lower corner of the image.

Until now, creators were forced to manually crop out edges—sacrificing composition and aspect ratios—or use lossy AI inpainting tools that smudge surround pixels. 02. The Science: Reverse Alpha Blending Algorithm

Unlike general-purpose photo editors that try to guess what pixels are beneath an obstacle by taking nearby color samples, Google Gemini watermarks are applied using standard linear alpha compositing. In computer graphics, when a translucent overlay logo \(C_{logo}\) with an alpha transparency \(\alpha\) is placed on top of an original pixel \(I_{original}\), the resulting watermarked pixel \(I_{watermarked}\) is calculated as:

\(I_{watermarked} = (\alpha \cdot C_{logo}) + ((1 - \alpha) \cdot I_{original})\)

Because the exact color \(C_{logo}\) and opacity map \(\alpha\) of the Gemini sparkle logo are constant and known, we can algebraically invert the composite blending formula to solve directly for the original un-watermarked pixel \(I_{original}\):

\(I_{original} = \frac{I_{watermarked} - (\alpha \cdot C_{logo})}{1 - \alpha}\)

By applying this exact equation across every pixel covered by the logo, our algorithm recovers the authentic background colors with 100% losslessness. There is zero blur, zero distortion, and zero loss of resolution.
